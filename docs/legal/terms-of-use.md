# Gozali Terms of Use

> Draft for the pilot. It must be reviewed before the public launch. Published at gozali.app/terms.

Last updated: September 27, 2026

By using Gozali you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app. Contact: hello@gozali.app.

## Who can use Gozali

You must be at least **13 years old**. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

## Your content

- You own the photos and text you post. You give Gozali permission to store them and show them to the members of your pack, and to include them in your pack's weekly recap, only to run the app.
- Post only photos you took yourself for your habit. Don't post photos of other people without their permission.

## What is not allowed

- Offensive, hateful, harassing or threatening content, and bullying of any kind, including through nudges, reactions or names.
- Nudity, sexual content, violence, self-harm content or illegal content.
- Spam, impersonation, or trying to get around the app's rules or its security.

Captions and names are checked for offensive words, and some text will be refused.

## Reports and removal

Every photo can be reported from its menu. We review reports **within 24 hours**. We may hide content, and we may remove members or delete accounts that break these terms. Several reports on the same photo hide it until it is reviewed. You can also block any member, and any member can leave a pack at any time; a pack admin can remove members.

## The game

The pet, its health, the streak and the achievements are part of a game with no monetary value. Rules and numbers may change as we tune the game. Version 1 has no payments.

## Availability

We work to keep Gozali running, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of errors. The app is provided "as is".

## Ending

You can delete your account at any time in Settings. We may suspend or end access for anyone who breaks these terms.

## Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
